Dates for decided applications

Only the retrospective re-scoring of applications decided in a date range reads these two dates.

Which settings the run reads depends on the cases you include.

  • Synthetic year (sample data): the seed, the number of cases, and the narrowing by permit type, district and business category.
  • Current applications: the narrowing by permit type and district. The seed and the number of cases do not apply.
  • Applications decided in a date range: the dates, and the narrowing by permit type and district. The seed and the number of cases do not apply.
